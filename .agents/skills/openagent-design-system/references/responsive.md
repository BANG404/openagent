# Responsive behavior

Preserve each surface's existing breakpoints and container constraints. The remote
conversation uses the desktop presentation settings and its configured two-column
threshold; embedded Settings/plugin cards wrap against their actual surface width.
Do not apply marketing-site navigation, full-screen hero or store-grid breakpoints
to the application shell.

Check narrow and wide affected surfaces, long Chinese/English labels, enlarged
text, internal scrolling and visible footer actions. Native onboarding keeps its
fixed window geometry; its body and form columns scroll without resizing that window.

Controls retain usable pointer/touch targets and visible keyboard focus. Collapse
secondary navigation without removing the path to its actions. Follow
[layout](layout.md) for bounded scroll regions and [product presentation](overview.md)
for shell/onboarding geometry.
